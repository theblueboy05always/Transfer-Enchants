
# Transfer Enchants — Troubleshooting

## Before reporting a problem

1. Confirm your Minecraft version.
2. Confirm your Fabric Loader version.
3. Confirm your Transfer Enchants version.
4. Restart the game and check whether the issue persists.
5. If safe and practical, test with only the required mods.
6. Back up your world before conducting further tests.

## The game crashes

Attach the crash report and describe what you were doing when the crash occurred.

## The forge interface is blank or incorrectly textured

Attach a screenshot and include the mod version and any relevant errors from the game log.

## Items disappear from the forge

Check the player inventory and document the exact steps that caused the issue. Include whether the issue happens when inserting, transferring, or closing the interface.

## The crystal or enchantment transfer behaves unexpectedly

Describe the source item, its enchantments, the target item, the crystal's condition, and what happened.

## A compatibility issue occurs

List the other mods installed and explain how to reproduce the conflict.
